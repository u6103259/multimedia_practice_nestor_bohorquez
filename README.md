User profile:
The final user will be someone that's looking for an engineering student to hire,
without experience probably it would be an intern in to a company.

Information architecture:
The page is a single page layout split into two columns, with a "card"
for each block of information. I grouped the content by purpose, so a recruiter
can scan it quickly from top/left to bottom/right.

The left column is the personal side. 
The first card is the profile: photo, name, degree, location, phone and email. 
It comes first because a recruiter needs to know who I am and how to contact me. 
The second card contains "About me" and "Skills". Both are about my personality, so I put them
together. The skills listed are soft skills: cooperation, communication,
adaptability, which is important to my performance in a job in general.

The right column is the professional side, in a single card with three
sections: Education, Experience and Learned. Education shows where I was educated, 
from primary school to the university degree.
Experience should show where I worked, without anything it just pretends to
search for an intern job or similar. To compensate, "Learned"
lists the languages and tools I have studied (C++, Python, Java, HTML, CSS,
JavaScript, Linux), which is what a company hiring a computer engineering
intern wants to see.

The reading direction goes from left to right, and from general to specific, 
so the most important information is always visible first.

Visual design:
First of all, I was inspired by the GNOME style. I took from it the minimalism
and the ease of reading. To achieve that, I used a similar dark grey #212121
and rounded corners to make the page soft and pleasant to look at. I also took
the "islands" idea, where each block is a separate card, from the JetBrains
IDE themes.

The palette is small on purpose. I wanted a minimalist result, so it is
mainly two colors: #212121 for the page background and the cards, and #100D08
for the container behind them. This darker tone makes the cards be the protagonist and
gives the page a bit of elegance. All the text is pure white #FFFFFF, which
gives a high contrast and makes it very easy to read on a dark background.

The layout is a two-column grid with a 15px gap between cards. This spacing
keeps each block visually separated, so the information can be scanned quickly.
I used two different border radius values, a smaller one for the container and
a bigger one for the cards, to create a good-looking visual hierarchy.

About the typography, there is not much to say. At first I wanted to use Inter,
but that required adding an external font, so I decided to use the
default monospace font, which is easy to read and fits a technical profile. I
also decided not to use icons, to keep the design simple.
